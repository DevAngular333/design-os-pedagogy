## DevAngular333 contribution

The `DevAngular333` fork adds a standard-library end-to-end smoke runner at
[`scripts/smoke_flow.py`](scripts/smoke_flow.py), a reproducible public demo
path, and the Vietnamese case study in [`docs/case-study.md`](docs/case-study.md).
The runner exercises the authored rehearsal through its actual loopback HTTP
server, SQLite-backed session transitions, replay check, and Markdown export.

The rehearsal content and coaching remain authored simulation data. They are not
semantic grading, expert review, a user study, or a teaching certification.
